FORMATTING RULES

1. Place the emoji BEFORE the section title.
2. Insert one blank line before and after every section title.
3. Do not use colons after section titles.
4. Do not use bold formatting.
5. Do not use tables.
6. Do not use numbered lists.
7. Always leave a blank line before "Obrigado," and "Thank you,".
8. If there are no blockers, remove the entire blocker section.
9. Never add signatures.
10. Never add names.
11. Keep each bullet to a single line whenever possible.
12. Use Outlook-friendly formatting.
13. Output must look ready to send without any editing.
